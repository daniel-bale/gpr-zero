# Category Mapping Analysis: Consolidating GPR Categories into 8 Representative Groups

## Overview
This document analyzes how the 172 unique categories found across all GPR dictionaries can be mapped to 8 representative categories. This consolidation enables stable prompt generation while maintaining comprehensive coverage of geopolitical risk dimensions.

## Rationale for Category Selection

### Why "Cross-Strait Political Tensions" is NOT a separate category
In recent years, cross-strait political and diplomatic tensions rarely occur in isolation - they almost always come with military escalation. Pure political rhetoric without military backing does not constitute a significant geopolitical risk in the current environment. Additionally, political tensions alone don't qualify as hybrid warfare and are difficult to justify as a standalone risk category. Therefore, cross-strait political elements are incorporated into Military Escalation where they manifest as actual threats.

## Validation Summary

The 8 representative categories successfully capture all major dimensions of geopolitical risk:
- **Coverage**: All 172 unique categories can be mapped to one or more of the 8 categories
- **Balance**: Each representative category has substantial mapped subcategories
- **Clarity**: The categories are distinct enough to avoid excessive overlap
- **Comprehensiveness**: Together they cover military, political, economic, diplomatic, technological, and strategic dimensions

## Rationale for Selection

1. **Military Escalation**: Core traditional security threat (includes cross-strait military tensions)
2. **Internal Political Instability**: Domestic risks that can be exploited by adversaries
3. **Economic Coercion**: Growing importance of economic statecraft
4. **Diplomatic Isolation**: Critical for Taiwan's international space
5. **Cyber and Information Warfare**: Emerging domain of conflict
6. **US-China-Taiwan Relations**: Essential trilateral dynamics
7. **Regional Security**: Broader strategic context
8. **Gray Zone Operations**: Captures modern hybrid threats

This consolidation enables consistent dictionary generation while maintaining comprehensive risk coverage. The decision to merge cross-strait tensions into military escalation reflects the reality that political tensions without military backing rarely constitute significant geopolitical risk in the current Taiwan context.
